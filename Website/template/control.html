Placing the player sensor mode

Kalman off: each node's distance goes median → FFT, and line of sight puts the player at the newest reading, with no gate. The servos do not use it.

Angle limit on: a node's reading further than its servo line runs on the 150 × 160 cm grid (+20 %) is dropped by line of sight and Dynamic's rules; trilateration still uses it.

Tri aim tolerance on: trilateration keeps the two nodes' crossing when a servo points up to 20° further off it than its beam; off, it falls back to the nearer node on its own.

Cell lock on: the game's cursor stays inside the cell the vote decided, and only that cell's hole scores, until the vote moves. Off: the cursor goes wherever the position does and scores whichever hole it is over.

Cell decision on: the cell moves once the position is 6 cm past its edges for 0.5 s, and a node that still sees the player where it did before counts for staying, so a scan turn changing over is not a move. Off: the older vote (13 of the last 25 readings).

Tracking (off by default): while the player is moving (twice in a row, a node sees them 15 cm from where it did two readings before, or they go 40 cm/s), the cell moves after 0.2 s instead of 0.5 s; still again after 0.7 s, or at once when a node has held them within 10 cm for 5 readings.

Too close the 10 cm dead zone at the nodes

Dead zone on: too close when a node's raw reading, along its servo line, is less than 10 cm out from the nodes (the 150 × 10 cm strip at the front of the grid): 10 cm straight ahead, 29 cm with the servo turned 70°. Off: the raw reading under 10 cm, whatever the angle. Hold the button to show the too-close screen; lift your finger to go back.

Dynamic's rules checked in this order

--

Far priority: when both nodes have read past 110 cm in the last 1.5 s (one head is enough) and the two readings agree - they cross where both servos point - the player is there (FAR), and not Out of bounds, whatever the no-echo and stray nearer readings in between say. A node that finds you nearer twice in a row ends it. Confidence: a node whose Confidence (the Sensors table below) is at or above the level places the player on its own, along its servo line, and the other node is left out (CONF L, CONF R); both at it, the higher one. −/+ moves the level 5 %. Far corners: in A1 (back left) or A3 (back right), the near node places the player on its own when it is sure of them (Dynamic says A1 / A3). Column lock: where the two servo lines cross says the column - the centre (held 1 s), or at least 20 cm into a side column (MID, COL L, COL R). Lone node: when only one node is sure of the player, it places them on its own (L, R).

Confidence: the share of a node's readings in the last 5 s that put the player in one spot, ± how tightly. Green is confident (80 %, for handover); underlined is at Dynamic's confidence level; "following n" is a node being aimed at node n's fix. Handover off: confidence is still worked out, but no node is aimed (no LOOK).

Far hold: after an echo 100 cm or more out, a node keeps its servo still for up to 3 lost pairs before it sweeps (not at its servo's stop). Far steer: a half-found echo 100 cm or more out steers the servo 1° instead of 3°. Both are in the nodes' firmware: they need the firmware from 6 Oct on, and work with no game page open.
